HookScene

Automated Microdrama Promo Discovery & Intelligence Studio
1. Problem Selection & Operational Context The Bottleneck

Microdrama platforms (ReelShort, DramaBox, Kuku) depend heavily on short vertical video ads across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts to acquire paying subscribers. Because studios release new 50-episode series weekly, selecting high-converting promo clips manually has become a major operational bottleneck: video editors spend hours watching raw episodes by hand.

I chose Problem 1 because it solves an immediate business bottleneck with deterministic, verifiable criteria. A high-converting promo requires four non-negotiable qualities:

Creative Attributes of a High-Yield Promo

• 3-Second Scroll-Stopping Hook: Starts directly at an intense emotional peak (altar betrayal, disownment, slap, or drawn weapon) within the first 3.0 seconds.

• Unresolved Cliffhanger: Cuts abruptly at peak tension right before revelation, creating an irresistible open loop that compels viewers to watch the episode.

Technical & Editorial Constraints

• Standalone Clarity: A first-time viewer instantly understands the central dramatic conflict without requiring prior backstory.

• Safe Dialogue Boundaries: Strictly adheres to 20–60s duration, with cut points snapped to script dialogue boundaries to prevent clipping speech mid-sentence.

2. Solution Overview: HookScene Studio The Approach

HookScene is a promo discovery system and interactive editorial review desk. Instead of requiring editors to manually watch 50 episodes, HookScene ingests raw screenplays, detects high-retention turning points, enforces duration and safe-cut rules, and outputs ranked promo candidates ready for single-click review and NLE export.

Core Architectural Principle: AI Finds the Drama, Pure Code Enforces the Rules

The language model is strictly confined to analyzing character stakes and emotional tension; it is strictly forbidden from writing timestamps. Deterministic Python code derives all cut points from screenplay timestamps, guarantees dialogue boundary safety, and strictly validates duration and hook constraints.

3. How the System Works: 6-Stage Execution Pipeline Architecture
Stage 1
Ingestion & Tokenization
Regex parses episodes into discrete Line entities with stable IDs and maps safe dialogue boundaries (cut_ok).
Stage 2
Arc & Anti-Spoiler Shield
Maps series arc and consensus-locks climax episodes (Eps 44–50) to prevent major reveals in promos.
Stage 3
Dramatic Peak Discovery
AI scans dialogue for betrayals and cliffhangers. Keyword heuristic acts as parallel zero-cost backstop.
Stage 4
Deterministic Code Gates
Python verifies duration (20–60s), hook onset (≤3.0s), and dialogue safety. Rejections trigger auto-repair windowing.
Stage 5
Multi-Rubric Scoring
Dual-judge rates candidates on 5 rubrics. Attention proxy adds immediacy. Borda count deduplicates similar beats.
Stage 6
Montage & NLE Timeline
Fuses high-intensity cross-episode beats to cut dead air. Exports CMX 3600 EDL, Final Cut XML, and JSON.

HookScene — Architecture, Evaluation & Verification

5. Engineering Approach, Scalability & Cost Implementation

Why the AI Never Writes Timestamps: Early prototypes that asked models to write timestamps directly produced frequent math errors, hallucinated clip durations, and cut actors off mid-sentence. HookScene enforces a strict boundary: the model only returns line IDs. Deterministic Python code resolves line IDs to exact screenplay timestamps, enforcing duration constraints and dialogue pause boundaries reliably.

Parallel Scalability & Speed

Episode parsing and candidate discovery run asynchronously in parallel. Processing all 50 episodes of a show takes ~2 minutes 45 seconds (~106 API calls) on the fast profile.

Provider Agnostic & Cost

A decoupled provider interface in config.yaml allows instant swapping between Gemini, Groq, or Ollama. A full 50-episode run costs $0.00 on Gemini Free Tier or ~$0.18 (₹16) on paid tiers.

7. Real Limitations & The Role of Human Review Honest Assessment
Where the AI System Has Blind Spots

• Text vs Video Reality: Analyzes screenplay text, not rendered video; subtle glares, micro-expressions, or physical comedy cannot be measured by text alone.

• Lower-Rank Score Drift: Top 10 candidates are highly stable, but ranks 20–30 exhibit natural LLM score variance (0.5–1.0 point drift).

• Timestamp Alignment: Assumes screenplay timestamps accurately reflect final edited video footage.

Why the Human Editor Remains Essential

• Editorial Multiplier: HookScene replaces manual scrubbing. Instead of watching 50 episodes (2+ hours), editors review the top 10 candidates in under 5 minutes.

• Creative & Compliance Discretion: Human judgment is essential to judge comedic delivery, cultural nuances, and platform-specific advertising compliance policies.

A Real Bug Diagnosed & Fixed: The Episode Number Collision
1. The Root Problem

An AI regex in parse.py strictly expected EPISODE NUMBER: <num>. Uploaded scripts with headers like TEST-01 failed extraction and defaulted to Ep 00.

2. The Silent Failure

In Python's episode map em[e.num] = e, each subsequent file silently overwrote earlier episodes, outputting promo candidates only from the final file.

3. The Permanent Fix

Replaced rigid regex with multi-pattern prefix matching, added filename fallback extraction, and added an auto-indexer in load_show to guarantee zero overwrites.
